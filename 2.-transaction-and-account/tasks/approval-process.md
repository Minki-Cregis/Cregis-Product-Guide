# Approval Process

When a transaction triggers a Policy, and the current account is assigned as an approver with approval permissions, the transaction will be routed to Tasks > Approvals. Approvers can choose to approve or reject the transaction. Cregis supports both single and batch approvals to help teams process pending transactions efficiently. Once processed, you can view the approval history under Tasks > Approved.

**Single Approval**

Navigate to Tasks > Approval, find the target transaction, and click Revie on the right side.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1123).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

On the approval details page, review the transaction details. Based on your review, select:

* **Approve**: Authorize the transaction to proceed.
* **Reject**: Decline the transaction, terminating it immediately.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1124).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Click Approve or Reject. The system will prompt you for identity verification—enter your Transaction Password and click Confirm.

Upon successful verification, a confirmation message will appear.

* **If approved**: The transaction moves to the next stage in the process.
* **If rejected**: The transaction is terminated.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1125).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Batch Approval**&#x20;

When handling multiple pending transactions, use batch approval to streamline the process.

Navigate to Tasks > Approvals and click **Batch Process** in the top right corner.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1126).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Select the transactions you wish to process and choose an action:

* **Approve**: Authorize the selected transactions to proceed.
* **Reject**: Decline the selected transactions, terminating them immediately.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1127).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

_(Optional)_ Enter any operational notes in the remarks pop-up window, then click Confirm.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1128).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Complete the identity verification by entering your Transaction Password and clicking Confirm. A success notification will appear once verified.

* If **approved**: The transactions move to the next stage.
* If **rejected**: The transactions are terminated.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (1129).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
