# Why Can’t My Team Account Be Upgraded?

Your team may be unable to upgrade due to the following issue.

## There is an unpaid order, so a new order cannot be submitted.

If you encounter this situation, you will see the error code screen below. You can cancel the original order and then submit a new order.

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (34).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Please go to the Orders page of your account. The billing status will be shown as "to be paid." You can click on the bill to enter the details page.&#x20;

<figure><img src="../../../.gitbook/assets/image (35).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

If you confirm that you want to cancel the bill, click "Cancel Order."
