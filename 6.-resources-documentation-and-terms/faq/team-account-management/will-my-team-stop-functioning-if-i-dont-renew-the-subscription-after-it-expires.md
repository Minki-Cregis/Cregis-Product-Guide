# Will My Team Stop Functioning If I Don’t Renew the Subscription After It Expires?

The Cregis team plan provides a 7-day grace period after expiration. If the renewal is not completed within 7 days, the upgraded services will be terminated. If you have used capacity expansion services, the related services will no longer function properly after expiration. For example, if you created two multi-signature wallets during the upgrade, the use of these wallets will be restricted after expiration. To avoid affecting your operations, please be sure to renew as soon as possible before the expiration date.

To avoid disruptions to your daily operations caused by forgetting to renew, we recommend that you enable the [auto-renewal service](../../../2.-transaction-and-account/images-and-media/manage-your-pricing-plan.md#auto-renewal).
