# Why Does the Shard Not Support Adding Tokens?

Different tokens within a blockchain have varying technical requirements. The shard architecture of newly integrated chains may be incompatible with the original chain, requiring a recalculation of the shard to add the token.

If you encounter this issue, simply <mark style="color:green;">**reset the shard**</mark>. For detailed steps on resetting shards, please refer to [here](../../../3.-wallet-and-risk-control/shard/#reset-a-shard).
